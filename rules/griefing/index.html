3. Griefing & Land Rules

3.1 Griefing Near Claims

Destroying or damaging terrain within or near claimed land is not allowed.

Examples include:

  • Lava or water spam structures
  • TNT craters
  • Excessive entity spam
  • Structures made only to annoy or disrupt towns
  • Damage around another town's borders

3.2 Wilderness Damage

Unclaimed land can be modified freely, as long as it does not negatively affect nearby towns or important infrastructure.

Major terrain damage may be rolled back by staff.

3.3 Claim Abuse

Claiming land purely to block another town from expanding is not allowed.

Long claim arms, disconnected claim patterns, or claims designed only to control land unfairly may be removed by staff.

3.4 Valuable Block Removal

Taking valuable blocks is allowed if it does not count as intentional griefing or unnecessary destruction.

3.5 Major Terraforming

Large-scale terrain changes that alter the natural look of the Earth map are not allowed.

Examples include:

  • Draining oceans, lakes, or rivers
  • Creating large artificial islands
  • Covering oceans with platforms
  • Mass reshaping natural geography

Vertical building is allowed as long as it does not heavily damage the natural map.

3.6 Map Art

Map art is allowed if it does not interfere with other players, nearby towns, or natural terrain.

Map art must not contain offensive, explicit, racist, or rule-breaking content.
