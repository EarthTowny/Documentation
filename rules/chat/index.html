2. Behaviour & Chat

2.1 Naming Rules

  • Nation names should reflect real-world locations, regions, or historical places.
  • Town names must be appropriate and non-offensive.
  • Usernames, nicknames, skins, and titles must not be explicit, offensive, or promotional.

2.2 Raiding & Theft

Stealing and raiding are part of gameplay and are allowed. Players are responsible for protecting their own resources.

2.3 Spam

Repeated or excessive messaging that disrupts chat flow is not allowed.

2.4 Inappropriate Behaviour

The following is prohibited:

  • Harassment, bullying, or toxic behaviour
  • Hate speech or discrimination
  • Encouraging self-harm or violence
  • Sexual or explicit discussions
  • Manipulating, grooming, or exploiting other players

Staff decisions on behaviour are final. Repeated offences may lead to stronger punishments.

2.5 Impersonation

Pretending to be another player, town, nation, or staff member in a misleading way is not allowed.

2.6 Personal Information

Sharing private information about others without permission is strictly forbidden. This includes real names, addresses, phone numbers, social media accounts, schools, workplaces, family information, and IP addresses.

2.7 Links

Malicious or harmful links, including viruses or IP grabbers, are not allowed. Links that break other rules are also prohibited.

2.8 Advertising

Advertising external servers, communities, or services is not allowed unless directly related to EarthTowny and approved by staff.

2.9 Real Money Trading

Selling or exchanging in-game items, services, currency, towns, or accounts for real money is not allowed.

2.10 Language

Global chat should primarily be in English. Other languages may be used in private messages, town chat, nation chat, or local channels.
